How does this GPS system work?

  1. Set Site GPS: Acquire GPS coordinates from the button above while standing on site.
  2. Add Worker: Add plumbers, supervisors, or mistri and share their tracking link on WhatsApp.
  3. Workers open link on phone: Workers open the link once to allow location. The app automatically checks them in within radius and checks out when leaving.
  4. Working hours calculated automatically: Total working hours will be tracked live in the table above.
